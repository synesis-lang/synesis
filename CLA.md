## Contributing on behalf of an employer

If you are contributing on behalf of an employer — including work done on
company time or equipment — your employer may own the copyright, in which
case your personal acceptance of this Agreement alone cannot grant the
rights described above. A **Corporate CLA (CCLA)**, accepted by someone
authorized to act for the company, is required in addition to this
Agreement. This is a one-time step per company, not per person or per pull
request: once a company is on file, every current and future contributor
from that company is cleared automatically.

If your company is already on file, you will not notice any of this. If it
is not, open an issue in the [synesis](https://github.com/synesis-lang/synesis)
repository titled `CCLA: <company name>` and the Maintainer will send you
the document.

Not sure whether this applies to you? Open the pull request anyway and ask.
It is a common question and never a problem.
